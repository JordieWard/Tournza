    Privacy Policy

    Last updated: 12 September 2026

    This policy reflects how Tournza handles your data. Please keep it up to date if your practices change; it is provided as a general policy and not as legal advice.

    1. Who we are

    Tournza ("we", "us") runs online regional darts events for players in the United Kingdom. Tournza is operated by Jordan Ward, who is the data controller responsible for your personal data. For any privacy question or to exercise your rights, contact admin@tournza.co.uk. We are based in the United Kingdom.

    2. What we collect

    • Account details when you sign up: your name, email address, and a password (stored securely and never visible to us — see Section 4).
    • Details you give us through the join/contact form: your name, email address, region, the reason you're contacting us and any message.
    • Your confirmation that you are 18 or over.
    • Event registrations, results, and rankings data, and your membership status.
    • Fair-play verification status (age check and cam check) once completed.
    • Payment is handled by our payment provider (Stripe). We receive confirmation of payment and, for members, an ongoing subscription reference so membership can renew automatically — but we do not collect or store your card details ourselves.

    3. How we use it and our lawful basis

    • To run your account, membership, and event entries — lawful basis: performance of a contract.
    • To verify eligibility and protect fair play — lawful basis: legitimate interests in running fair, secure competition.
    • To send you service emails — confirming a payment, entry, or password reset — lawful basis: performance of a contract / legitimate interests in operating the service.
    • To send you optional updates about events — lawful basis: your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

    4. Who we share it with, and where it's stored

    We do not sell your data. Your account and event data is stored in a hosted database provided by Supabase (which also handles secure sign-in — your password is encrypted and never visible to us or anyone else), not on a local device. We use a small number of other processors to deliver the service:

    • Supabase — hosts our database and handles account sign-in.
    • Stripe — processes membership and event payments, and manages your subscription if you're a member.
    • Formspree — transmits join/contact form submissions to us.
    • Resend — delivers service emails (payment confirmations, entry confirmations, password resets) on our behalf.

    We may disclose data if required by law.

    5. International transfers

    Our processors (Supabase, Stripe, Formspree, and Resend) may process or store data outside the UK, including in the United States or EU. Where they do, they rely on appropriate safeguards such as UK-approved standard contractual clauses, as set out in their own terms.

    6. How long we keep it

    We keep your personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes above and to meet our legal, accounting and regulatory obligations under applicable UK legislation, after which it is securely deleted. Records tied to payments are retained for the period required by law.

    7. How we protect it

    Your data is held in encrypted, access-controlled systems provided by our processors above, protected by row-level access rules so accounts can only access their own data. Passwords are encrypted by our authentication provider and are never visible to us. Card details never touch our systems — Stripe handles these directly. We review access regularly.

    8. Your rights

    Under UK GDPR you can ask us to access, correct, delete, restrict or port your data, and object to certain processing. You can withdraw consent at any time. To make a request, email admin@tournza.co.uk. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk.

    9. Cookies and similar storage

    This site does not use tracking or marketing cookies. Signing in uses your browser's local storage to keep you logged in — this is strictly necessary for the account and membership features to work, not for tracking, and doesn't require separate cookie consent. If we add analytics or marketing cookies later, we will update this policy and seek consent where required.

    10. Children

    Tournza is for players aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from under-18s.

    11. Changes

    We may update this policy and will change the date above when we do.

    Fair play

    Fair Play Rules

    Tournza runs on trust and a clear camera. These rules keep every regional event fair. By entering an event you agree to follow them.

    1. Setup & equipment

    • Regulation board: bullseye 1.73 m from the floor, played at the standard 2.37 m oche distance.
    • Use a steel-tip or soft-tip board appropriate to the event listing, in good condition with clear numbers.

    2. Camera

    • Your webcam must show both the board and your throwing action clearly for the whole match.
    • Keep the board well lit and unobstructed. Don't move or re-angle the camera mid-leg.
    • Officials may ask for a quick camera recheck at any time.

    3. Match play

    • 501, double-out. Events are a group round robin into a knockout to the final, capped at 32 players.
    • Call your score after each visit; your opponent confirms. Flag any disagreement before throwing again.
    • Be checked in and ready before check-in closes (10 minutes before start) or your place may go to a reserve.

    4. Conduct

    • Respect opponents, officials and other players. Abusive or threatening behaviour isn't tolerated.
    • Play your own darts — no coaching, prompts or assistance during a leg.

    5. Integrity & anti-cheating

    • No score manipulation, editing the feed, or misrepresenting throws.
    • We run random checks. Suspected breaches are reviewed against the match recording.
    • Confirmed cheating can mean disqualification, forfeiting your buy-in and any prize, and suspension or a ban.

    6. Disputes

    Raise an issue with the official during the match, or email admin@tournza.co.uk afterwards with your event and the time of the incident. We review the recording where available; the official's final decision stands.

    7. Eligibility

    Events are for verified members aged 18 or over with a cleared age and camera check. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.
